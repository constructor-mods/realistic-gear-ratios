# Realistic Gear Ratios

In F1 25 every 2026 car has the same eight gears. This addon gives each team its own gearing, like the real 2026
grid: McLaren runs short gears, Audi the longest top gear, Mercedes long gears all the way up, Red Bull a long 1st.

- **What changes:** every 2026 car's gears, the player's car and the AI drivers'. The final drive stays the game's.
- **Settings:** **2026 team gear ratios** turns it on and off, straight away.

Works in Grand Prix and Time Trial with the 2026 cars. Driver Career, My Team and older seasons are left alone.
Offline only.
